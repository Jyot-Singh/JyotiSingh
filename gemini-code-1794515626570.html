I am a BIM Architect who loves exploring new ways to work. I have developed custom pyRevit scripts to automate manual, boring tasks that often lead to human errors. Here are a few samples.

1. Automated Room Finishes

The Manual Way: Tediously tracing the perimeter of every single room to individually model the skirting, floor layers, and wall plaster.
The Solution: Read the room boundaries and instantly generate all architectural finishes in one click.

2. Automated Room Numbering

The Manual Way: Clicking each room one by one, typing the number, realizing you missed one, and having to fix the entire sequence by hand.
The Solution: Select the space, define the format, and correctly number all rooms across the floor in one click.

3. Auto-Dimensioning Tool

The Manual Way: Clicking every single wall face, struggling to match the spacing between all the dimension lines, zooming in and out, and obsessively readjusting.
The Solution: Decide your spacing once and generate perfectly aligned, standardized dimensions in one click.

4. Grid Alignment Utility

The Manual Way: Manually moving the grid on 50+ sheets, trying to decide which looks better, drawing temporary reference lines to check, and fighting to keep it consistent everywhere.
The Solution: Snap all grid heads and tails to a perfect, unified alignment across the entire project in one click.

5. Bulk Wall Type Management

The Manual Way: Tired of making 100+ wall types, duplicating them, renaming them, and going back and forth to update descriptions, keynotes, etc.
The Solution: Update everything in Excel, import all changes into Revit at once, and export it back to Excel to keep handy during client meetings.
